# Platform-Identification-System-using-Acoustic-Signature

## Dataset
The DeepShip dataset(Kaggle: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vasundharauppuluri/deep-ship) contains:

47 hours and 4 minutes of real‑world underwater acoustic recordings 
265 different ships recorded, belonging to four commercial classes:

Oil tanker
Tug
Passenger ship
Cargo ship

Recordings were collected between May 2016 and October 2018 in the Strait of Georgia delta node 

The dataset includes natural background noise, marine mammal sounds, and human activity noise, making it realistic for sonar classification tasks
